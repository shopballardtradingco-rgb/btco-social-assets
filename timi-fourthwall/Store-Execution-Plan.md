## Production file handoffs
The supplied images are references, not completed print masters. Dam Crew needs separate chest, back and lower-leg artwork in exact supplier templates, approved lettering and orange/navy palette. Sunshine needs transparent full-resolution artwork per print area, exact master sun retained and approved removal/placement of signature. Good morning and Hello sit above art; selected Positive Energy is its own approved sun. Mug artwork must face outward for both left- and right-handed users. Embroidery needs a simplified stitch proof, digitized machine file and physical sample preserving the recognizable sun. Do not auto-trace complex paint texture and call it embroidery-ready.
At 300 pixels/inch, a 12-inch print needs 3600 pixels at final size; actual required dimensions, file type, bleed and color handling come from the chosen supplier template. Save a vector master where genuinely vector-drawn; a PNG inside SVG is not vector art. Bracelet needs maker-engineered dimensions, CAD, thickness and attachment testing; illustration is not production CAD.
