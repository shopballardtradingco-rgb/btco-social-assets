## Three collections

- **Dam Crew:** navy, white and burnt-orange nautical cruise-inspired artwork; painted sun/waves, “Save Our Seats”; **no anchors**.
- **Timi Sunshine:** preserve Timi's exact vintage painted sun artwork. The ZIP has *Good morning*; the separate approved *Hello* and *Positive Energy* source art is still needed.
- **Camino:** continuous-line heart-and-arrow. Launch with a print-on-demand item; the physical bracelet needs separate maker/sample approval and is not a launch blocker.

## Fourthwall in plain English

Timi designs and approves; customers order from her store; Fourthwall's catalog production partners make, package and ship supported made-to-order products, while Fourthwall supports catalog order issues and payment checkout. Her owner account receives net payouts. This does **not** automatically cover a custom externally sourced bracelet.

## What to do next, for free

- Prepare but **do not distribute** the survey until Timi approves the questions and images.
- Prepare private product mockups for the three collections, with real Fourthwall catalog options and accurate cost estimates.
- Have Timi create her own store account and enter any tax, identity, or banking information privately.
- Show one or two simple choices at a time. Avoid unnecessary apps and do not spend, sample, make products public, accept minimum-order terms or start subscriptions without approval.

**Sustainability is a future possibility, not part of this launch's requirements.**
