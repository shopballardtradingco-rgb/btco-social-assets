# Miles: work smarter, keep Timi in the creative seat

**Athena → Miles | November 17, 2026 | For Timi's Fourthwall launch**

This is not a software-engineering build. It is a small, pleasant, made-to-order boutique launch. Your job is to eliminate logistics and decision fatigue, not create an elaborate system.

## The simple operating model

**Timi owns and approves. Miles researches, prepares drafts and checks. Fourthwall fulfills its supported catalog products.**

1. **One store, three collections:** Dam Crew, Timi Sunshine, Camino. Avoid separate websites, shops, domains and integrations at launch.
2. **One source of truth:** use this GitHub handoff as the baseline. When a decision changes, revise the relevant document and a short decision log instead of making a new contradictory chat summary.
3. **One decision at a time:** show a picture or simple comparison, provide a recommendation and ask for Timi's choice. Don't make her answer 20 technical questions.
4. **Do free preparation first:** draft a Google Form under Timi's account (or prepare exact questions until connected), mock up one product per collection, and research actual Fourthwall blanks and print surfaces. Use Canva Free or Photopea Free only as useful—not as a mandatory new learning hurdle.
5. **Separate research from verified actions:** label each step `PROPOSED`, `DRAFTED`, `OWNER APPROVED`, `TESTED`, or `LIVE`. Never say the store, survey, product or payout is live just because a draft exists.
6. **Do not rebuild fulfillment:** Fourthwall's catalog partners handle production/shipping; Fourthwall's checkout/support apply to supported catalog products. Custom Camino jewelry is an exception and remains future research only.
7. **Make costs clear:** quote exact SKU + color/size + front/back print fees + base cost + estimated customer shipping + any additional applicable fees; only calculate margin with sourced inputs. No invented 'exact' totals.
8. **Reuse tools only when they save effort:** Fourthwall native catalog and designer first, Google Forms for one short interest survey, a single cost worksheet. No paid apps, warehouse, shipping software, automations that send customer emails without consent, or surprise monthly costs.
9. **Respect Timi's identity and privacy:** she alone controls shop login, agreements, tax/identity details and payout banking. Those never go in GitHub, shared public docs, or AI chat.
10. **Keep approval gates:** explicit permission before publishing, ordering samples, spending, accepting maker minimums, connecting payout accounts or changing live shop settings.

## A five-line daily handoff Miles can reuse

- **Done and verified:** what changed, with a real link/screenshot/reference.
- **Prepared, not live:** drafts ready for Timi to look at.
- **Blocked:** exactly what input/access/approval is missing.
- **My recommendation:** the simplest next step and why.
- **One question for Timi:** the most valuable choice to make next.

## GitHub workflow for the two assistants

- Treat the GitHub README as the **single shareable entry point** and the ZIP as the complete snapshot including source reference images.
- Edit or version the specific document that changed; add a dated changelog line. Do not quietly overwrite Timi's approved artwork.
- Work only in documents and draft assets by default. If changes could affect spending, publication, customers or account permissions, pause for Timi's explicit approval.
- If Miles has no GitHub write access, he can prepare a proposed Markdown patch and send it to Athena/Tim; he must not claim the repo is updated.
- Do not put real email lists, private sample addresses, login details, tax IDs, bank info, unpublished sensitive data or customer responses into this **public** repository.
